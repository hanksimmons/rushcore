# Provisional decisions (parallel track)

P-numbers are proposals. They become accepted only when the main track promotes them to a D-number in
`docs/DECISIONS.md` with the user's verdict (`README.md` §8 in this directory). Until then the code on the `opus/`
branches implements them and every spec edit they cause carries the tag `(P-nnn)`.

Statuses: `PROPOSED` · `PROMOTED D-nnn` · `REJECTED` · `WITHDRAWN`.

Write one row per decision. Say what was chosen, the alternative, and why, in the DECISIONS.md style: dated, concrete
numbers, names of the code that carries it.

| P-001 | T1 | SUPERSEDED by P-010 (the user chose both carry, 2026-09-07) | Flow resets to 0 on stage entry; boost carries over. A stage is a chain boundary (02 §8 "falling/recovery ends Flow" is the nearest accepted rule); carrying boost keeps the pickup economy honest across the transition. Alternative: carry Flow. |

Recovery inside a stage still ends Flow, unchanged. |
| P-011 | T1 | THE USER'S DECISION (2026-09-07) | The exit the ball reaches picks the next stage's archetype: exit A continues the one just played, each further exit selects a different one, derived deterministically as `SeedChain.Derive(stageSeed, "route", exitIndex)` modulo the archetype count, re-rolled while it equals the current one so a fork always changes the landscape. | P-009 | T7 | PROPOSED (main track, 2026-09-07) | Tube judder while boosting: the shell collider goes from 10 to 24 facets per ring (even, so the bottom stays a corner and the cruise rests exactly on the circle), and the tube follow holds the collider's inscribed circle (R·cos(π/24) − ball) instead of the analytic one, so the collider never fires while the follow is active. Measured before on Highlands 9/0 with boost and the stick toward the wall for 3 s: contacts on 180 of 180 ticks, Δradial up to 15.7 cm per tick (rms 2.9 cm), penetration into the facet plane up to 27.8 cm (predicted 29.4), ride up to 95° off the bottom. After 24 sides + the inscribed circle alone: Δradial 5.7 cm, penetration 7.2 cm, contacts 175/180 (gravity, integrated after the callback, creeps the ball ≈ 6 cm into the wall before the closing velocity balances it), so the follow also supplies the wall's normal force in advance (gravity's outward component plus u²/r of the motion around the ring, × dt). The bound on the outward step comes before that pre-compensation, since the step carries `vOut + press·dt`. The hold margin is `2·deadband + TubeAxisUncertainty(5 cm) + 1 cm` inside the inscribed circle, sized by the measured error of the nearest-*vertex* axis reference the follow acts on (2.9–3.5 cm on a 4 m-sampled curving axis); the ball therefore floats about 12 cm off the glass, and the constant goes to zero once the structure query interpolates the axis (recorded for the main track). Result: penetration 0.0 cm (was 28), contacts under the follow 6 of 180 grazes at zero depth (was 180 of 180), radial motion 1.01 cm rms a tick (was 2.89), whose floor is the follow's shared 3 cm rest band. Alternative rejected: matching the polygon exactly (the ball would ride a 29 cm or 5 cm wobble per facet). |
| P-012 | T1 | PROPOSED (T1, 2026-09-07) | Two affordances the packet did not anticipate, both needed by the shape of the existing code. (a) `RunDirector.AutoAdvance` (default on): with it off, reaching an exit still raises `StageCompleted` and still prints, but no outro runs and no next stage is built. The harness needs it, because the Phase 2 generated-stage case drives to exit A and then keeps using that same stage for the tube ride, the branch ride and the node-growth block; without the flag every one of those would be driving a different stage. It is also the honest debug affordance for "play this stage again". (b) `PlayerPhysics.TeleportTo(position, keepChain)`: `ApplyTeleport` zeroes Flow, which is the recovery rule (02 §8) and must stay, so the transition teleport asks to keep the chain and nothing else does. That is the only way P-010 can hold, since every stage entry goes through a teleport. Neither touches a velocity rule or a compiled default. |

| P-013 | T4 | PROPOSED (T4, 2026-09-07) | The instance caps (1800 rocks, 900 crystals, 240 markers a stage) are a ceiling rather than the working number: at the shipped density 0.19 a stage lands at about 820 rocks and 450 crystals, so `World › Prop Density` still does something, and the caps bind only past about 0.6.
